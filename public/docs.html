      MAN 1 OVERVIEW
      vote.terminal(1)
    

    

      
SYNOPSIS
    vote.terminal --token $LAPTOP

DESCRIPTION
    vote.terminal is a community ballot shell for fixed-supply tokens on
    robinhood.chain. Each round opens a 72h voting window during which any
    wallet may cast exactly one signed vote. The candidate with the highest
    support enters the listing review queue for the following round.

    The system does not custody assets. Your wallet submits every
    transaction; vote.terminal only relays signed intents.

      MAN 4 FAQ
      frequently asked
    

    

      

        Do I pay gas to vote?
        
In this demo, no. On a live deployment the vote signature is submitted through your wallet and network fees may apply.

      

      

        Can I change my vote?
        
No — one address, one irreversible ballot per round. Keep it deliberate.

      

      

        Is voting anonymous?
        
Vote intents are pseudonymous: they appear as hashed addresses in the public log. Your IP is never stored.

      

      

        What decides the winner?
        
Support percentage (yes / total). Thresholds are published per round; ties resolve by early arrival.

      

      

        Does listing guarantee value?
        
No. Listing is community curation only. Please read the risk notice.
