Specific risks

      
Market risk

      
Demo tokens on the ballot can experience extreme price movement, illiquidity, and total loss within hours. Past support percentages do not predict future outcomes.

      
Transaction risk

      
Signatures are final. A mis-signed or mis-routed transaction cannot be recalled by vote.terminal or any intermediary.

      
Technical risk

      
Rounds may be delayed, degraded, or interrupted by network congestion, smart-contract edge cases, or force majeure. Interrupted votes are not guaranteed a retry.

      
Regulatory risk

      
Token listings may be subject to rules that change without notice. Compliance in your jurisdiction is your responsibility.

      
Wallet risk

      
Never share your secret phrase. Anyone with your keys controls your address. vote.terminal will never ask for them.


      
Before you act

      
Review the token contract directly, verify supply mechanics, and only ever transact with funds you can afford to lose entirely. When in doubt: do not sign.
