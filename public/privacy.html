1. Scope

      
This policy describes what vote.terminal ("we", "the service") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have when you open a ballot, cast a vote, or propose a token.


      
2. What we collect

      
We operate pseudonymously by design. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or IP address. The service stores:

      
    
        
  • public wallet addresses that submit or sign ballot intents;
    • 
        
  • the vote direction (YES / NO) and its timestamp, as relayed to the public log;
    • 
        
  • proposal metadata you choose to submit, including a public contact channel.
    • 
      


      
3. How we use it

      
    
        
  • operate the ballot, prevent double-voting, and publish tallies;
    • 
        
  • surface community favorites for listing review;
    • 
        
  • log dry technical events (page loads) that we may retain for up to 30 days without identifying information.
    • 
      


      
4. What we do not do

      
We do not sell data, do not run advertising trackers, and do not store private keys. Wallet signatures are produced inside your wallet and never touch our servers.


      
5. Public by default

      
Votes are public and reproducible on-chain. Pseudonymity is not anonymity: a wallet that is linked to your identity elsewhere may expose your participation. Vote only with an address you are comfortable revealing.


      
6. Data retention & access

      
Ballot intents live on-chain indefinitely and cannot be deleted. Logs of proposal metadata may be removed upon request to the address published in the footer.


      
7. Children

      
The service is not directed at anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.


      
8. Changes

      
We may revise this policy. Material changes are announced on the ballot feed with a new revision tag.
